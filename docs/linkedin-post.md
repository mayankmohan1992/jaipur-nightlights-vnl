## MAIN POST

Jaipur's glow has more than doubled since 2013.

I mapped the official VIIRS night-time lights (NASA/NOAA) over the Jaipur Development Authority's Master Plan area — the same eight months (Jan–Aug) every year, so this year is measured on equal footing with the rest.

📈 Mean radiance: 3.50 → 7.44 nW·cm⁻²·sr⁻¹ — up 113%
⏸️ 2013–2020: almost flat, +1.0% a year
🚀 2021–2025: +9.5% a year — a step change
🛰️ 2026 (Jan–Aug): +11.2% on 2025
🌐 The lit share of the plan rose from 55% to 86% — the glow spread from one core into the corridors and ring towns

Fastest-growing zones, 2013→2026: ZDP-15 +260%, ZDP-10 +259%, ZDP-08 +253%.

One honest note: Jaipur starts bright, so its percentage growth understates the change. By this measure, the fastest-changing districts in Rajasthan are the smaller ones — Sikar, Bharatpur, Karauli, Jhunjhunu — not Jaipur. That's saturation, not stagnation.

Source: VIIRS Day/Night Band (NASA/NOAA); Earth Observation Group, Payne Institute for Public Policy, Colorado School of Mines (Elvidge et al.), processed via Google Earth Engine. Annual composites run to 2025; 2026 is January–August.
